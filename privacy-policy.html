Privacy Policy for ReLeaf Pads

Effective Date: October 2026

ReLeaf Pads ("we", "our", or "us") is dedicated to protecting the privacy and personal data of our users. This Privacy Policy outlines how the ReLeaf Pads mobile application collects, uses, processes, and protects your information in strict adherence with Google Play Developer Policies and applicable data protection regulations.

1. Information We Collect

To provide rapid on-demand sanitary pad delivery services, we collect the following minimum necessary data:

  • Personal Contact Information: Name, mobile phone number, delivery address, and postal pincode to communicate order updates and deliver products to your doorstep.
  • Foreground Location Data: With your active explicit permission, the application accesses your foreground device GPS coordinates solely to pinpoint your delivery doorstep for urgent fulfillment and calculate accurate delivery routes. We do not track or collect background location data when the application is closed.
  • Transaction References: Order summary items, timestamps, payment statuses, and reference transaction IDs provided by our payment processor. We do not store credit/debit card numbers, CVVs, or UPI PINs on our servers.

2. Purpose & Use of Data

We process personal data strictly for:

  • Fulfilling and delivering sanitary pad orders to your specified location.
  • Assigning delivery partners and optimizing transit routes.
  • Sending real-time order confirmation, tracking updates, and invoices via WhatsApp and SMS.
  • Providing prompt customer care and order dispute resolutions.

3. Third-Party Service Providers

We partner with industry-standard, secure third-party platforms to execute essential services:

  • Razorpay: Secure, PCI-DSS compliant online payment processing.
  • WhatsApp Business API: Automated real-time customer transactional alerts.
  • Mapping Services: Distance and route estimation for delivery partners.

4. Data Retention & Security

All communication between the application and our servers is encrypted using Transport Layer Security (TLS/HTTPS). Stored database records are protected with strict access controls. We retain personal records only as long as necessary for order fulfillment and statutory tax compliance.

5. User Rights & Data Deletion

You have full ownership of your data. You may request account deletion or complete erasure of your personal data at any time by emailing us at support@releafpads.in or contact@navabharath.com with your registered mobile phone number. Deletion requests are processed within 7 business days.

6. Children's Privacy

The ReLeaf Pads application is intended for adult users. We do not knowingly solicit or collect personal information from children under the age of 13.
